# Objective

Document the complete research process, implementation methodology,
experimental evaluation, and project outcomes in a well-structured
research paper suitable for academic publication and final capstone
submission.

document.

# Why This Phase Exists

A successful project is not only about building a working system.

It must also demonstrate:

-   The research problem
-   The novelty
-   The methodology
-   Experimental validation
-   Reproducibility
-   Future research potential

# Final Notes

This phase represents the culmination of the entire project. Every
design decision, implementation choice, experiment, and evaluation from
the previous phases should be documented clearly, honestly, and
reproducibly. A well-written paper should not only explain what was
built, but also why each design decision was made, how it was validated,
what limitations remain, and how future researchers or developers can
extend the system.
